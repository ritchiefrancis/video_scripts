Mileage Tracking

Target 1:00 VO ≈ 129 words The log-it-live video British English

The brief: log journeys and let the app do the maths. The whole video is built around adding one trip live on camera — the page starts empty and that's fine; no seeded list needed. The tiles doing their thing after one entry is the demo. Business-vs-personal is handled in the voiceover (log business journeys, leave the school run out) — there's no toggle on screen. Audience is non-tax-savvy; no jargon, no tax lecture.

0:00–0:12 The hook — miles are money 29 words · ~12s

On screen

The Mileage page, fresh and empty: the four tiles at zero (Total Miles, Tax Deduction, Total Trips, Digital Records Synced). Caption: "Every business mile counts."

Voiceover

If you drive for work, every business mile counts towards your tax — and scribbled postcodes on the back of a receipt won't cut it. FileThat keeps a proper mileage log.

Footage note: no seeding for this video — load any demo scene to wipe, and the empty state is the opening shot.

0:12–0:30 Add a trip — the live moment 30 words · ~13s

On screen

Press Add Trip → the Add Mileage Entry form, filled in real time:
  • today's date · from Workshop — Sale · to Jewson — Stockport
  • miles 14 · vehicle Car · purpose Materials pickup
Save → the trip appears and the tiles tick up: 14 miles · £6.30 · 1 trip.

Voiceover

Logging one takes seconds: where you went, why, and the miles. Save — and FileThat works out what it's worth at HMRC's approved rates, forty-five pence a mile to start.

0:30–0:44 What to log — and what not to 28 words · ~12s

On screen

Reopen the form briefly and drop the Vehicle Type menu — Car, Motorcycle, Bicycle — then close without saving. Caption: "Business journeys only."

Voiceover

Cars, motorbikes, even bicycles — each has its own rate. Keep the log to business journeys: client visits, supplier runs, getting to jobs. The school run doesn't count, so don't log it.

0:44–0:55 Into your records — one click 28 words · ~12s

On screen

Press Sync on the new trip → its status flips to Synced and the Digital Records Synced tile reads 1. A glance at the Download button — don't press it.

Voiceover

One more click sends the trip into your digital records, so it's already there when your quarterly update comes round. Prefer a spreadsheet? Download the whole log any time.

0:55–1:02 Close — log it while it's fresh 14 words · ~7s

On screen

Caption: "Log it while it's fresh." End card: FileThat logo + filethat.co.uk.

Voiceover

Log trips while they're fresh — future-you, and your tax bill, will thank you.

Fixed claims — please don't paraphrase these

· "At HMRC's approved rates, forty-five pence a mile to start" — state it as what the app calculates, nothing more. No advice about who can claim mileage, the 10,000-mile taper, or mileage-versus-actual-costs; "to start" is as far as the taper gets mentioned.

· "The school run doesn't count" — keep the business-only guidance this light. No commuting rules, no HMRC definitions.

· No pricing in this video. If an edit wants a price line, lift the exact wording from Video 1 ("keeping your records is free") — nothing new.

Practicalities

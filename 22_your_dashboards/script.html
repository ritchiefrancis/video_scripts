Your Dashboards

Target 0:45 VO ≈ 110 words The at-a-glance video British English

The brief: Profit & Loss and Portfolio overviews in one video. The message is not "look at these charts" — it's that the dashboards are built from the same records you already keep, so they're always current with nothing extra to do. Two views, one idea. Audience is non-tax-savvy; no jargon.

0:00–0:08 The hook — where do I stand? 18 words · ~8s

On screen

A quick flash of the old way — a spreadsheet, a pile of statements — then cut straight to the dashboard loading. Keep it brisk; this video earns its 45 seconds.

Voiceover

Want to know how the business is doing without digging through paperwork? That's what your dashboards are for.

0:08–0:19 Profit & Loss — the quarter at a glance 24 words · ~11s

On screen

The Profit & Loss view for Q1: income £15,750, expenses £6,621.14, profit £9,128.86. Hold long enough for the three numbers to land; a gentle highlight on each as it's spoken.

Voiceover

Profit and Loss shows the quarter at a glance: £15,750 in, £6,621.14 out — £9,128.86 profit. One screen, and you know exactly where you stand.

0:19–0:35 Portfolio — the whole picture 36 words · ~16s

On screen

Switch to the Portfolio overview: three income sources side by side — Harper Building Services (the trade), the UK rental, and the Costa Blanca Apartment in Spain — each with its own totals.

Voiceover

Got more than one string to your bow? The Portfolio view lines them all up — the building trade, the UK rental, even the apartment in Spain — so you can see the whole picture in one place.

0:35–0:49 The closer — always current, nothing to do 31 words · ~14s

On screen

Add a ledger entry → cut back to the dashboard, the totals tick up → end card: FileThat logo + filethat.co.uk.

Voiceover

And here's the best bit: there's nothing to update. The dashboards are built from the same records you keep every week — so every number is already current, every time you look.

Fixed claims — please don't paraphrase these

· "Built from the same records you keep" — this is the video's one claim; do not strengthen it to "live bank data" or "synced with HMRC".

· The three figures in beat 2 (£15,750 / £6,621.14 / £9,128.86) must match the on-screen dashboard exactly — they're the same numbers viewers see in the quarterly-update videos.

· No pricing in this video, and no specific tax-saving promises.

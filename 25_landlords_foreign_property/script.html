Landlords: Foreign Property Quarterly

Target 1:30 VO ≈ 210 words The overseas landlord video British English

The brief: the foreign-property counterpart to Video 19 — same quarterly journey (log as you go → quarter ends → pre-filled → review and submit), with two things that only exist for overseas rentals: the country registration (each property is registered to its country, shown on the Foreign Properties page) and relief for foreign tax paid. The reassurance beat is the point of the video: the country handling and double-taxation side are automatic — the viewer just keeps the records. Demo property: "Costa Blanca Apartment", Spain, £700/month, Q1 2026-27. Audience is non-tax-savvy; the word "crystallisation" does not appear in this or any other video.

Fixed claims — please don't paraphrase these

· "Relief for the foreign tax shown automatically… the double-taxation side is handled for you" — keep as written. Do not strengthen to "you'll never pay tax twice" or any promise about the final tax outcome.

· "Give it a once-over, press submit" — the review step stays in the VO and on screen; never imply the update files itself without review.

· "The paperwork lands in the right place automatically" refers to the property's country registration only — don't widen it into a claim about foreign tax rules generally.

· No pricing in this video, and no specific tax-saving promises.

Income & Expenditure Ledger

Target 1:00 VO ≈ 134 words The how-to video British English

The brief: free ledger basics — add income and expenses, watch the running totals. This is the sibling of Video 15: that one is the use case (export, hand to your accountant, 30 seconds); this one is the how-to (a minute of actually keeping the books). The centrepiece is adding an entry live on camera. Audience is non-tax-savvy; warm, practical, zero jargon — bookkeeping for people who'd rather be working.

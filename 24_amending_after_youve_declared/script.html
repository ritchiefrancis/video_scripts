Amending After You've Declared

Target 0:45 VO ≈ 105 words The reassurance video British English

The brief: pure reassurance, kept calm and short. The viewer has signed off their year (final declaration done) and then found a mistake. Message: while HMRC's amendment window is open, you can reopen the year, fix the record, and resubmit — and it's the same review-and-submit flow they already know from Video 6. No drama, no penalty talk, no jargon. Audience is non-tax-savvy.

0:00–0:11 The hook — mistakes happen 25 words · ~11s

On screen

Self Assessment, tax year 2025–26 — the year shown declared / signed off (final declaration complete). Calm, steady shot; nothing flashing, nothing red.

Voiceover

You've signed off your year — and then you spot a receipt down the back of the van. Don't panic. Mistakes happen, and HMRC knows it.

0:11–0:23 Reopen the year 27 words · ~12s

On screen

The Intent to Amend action on the declared 2025–26 year → confirm → the year reopens for corrections. If the amendment-window wording is visible on screen, let it be read.

Voiceover

If HMRC's amendment window is still open, you can put it right. Reopen the year in FileThat, and it's ready for corrections — no forms, no phone calls.

0:23–0:38 Fix the record, same flow as ever 34 words · ~15s

On screen

Correct the record (e.g. add the missed expense to the digital records) → the year's figures update → Calculate & Submit → review the returned calculation → Submit. The calculation shots reuse the staged 2025–26 figures (£36,750 profit, £6,286.80 due) — don't mock up altered numbers.

Voiceover

Fix the record itself — add what was missing, correct what was wrong — and the year's figures update to match. Then it's the same flow you already know: review the new calculation, and press submit.

0:38–0:46 Closer 19 words · ~8s

On screen

Confirmation / proof of submission for the resubmitted year → end card: FileThat logo + filethat.co.uk.

Voiceover

That's it — the record's right, and HMRC has the corrected year. One mistake doesn't undo a year's good work.

Fixed claims — please don't paraphrase these

· "If HMRC's amendment window is still open" — keep the conditional. Never imply a declared year can always be reopened, or state how long the window lasts.

· "Review the new calculation, and press submit" — the review step stays in the VO and in frame; never suggest the amendment goes to HMRC without the viewer reviewing it.

· Fix the record, not the form — corrections are made to the digital records and the figures follow. Don't reword to "edit the numbers on the return".

· Keep the tone consequence-free: no penalty or fine language anywhere in this video. No pricing either.

Practicalities

  • Total VO ≈ 105 words — lands around 0:45 at a relaxed pace, right on target. Nothing here should be rushed; if it runs long, slow the screens, not the voice.

Tax Planner

Target 0:45 VO ≈ 110 words The put-money-aside video British English

The brief: the Tax Planner turns "how much should I put aside?" into a number. Show the in-year position (one quarter of records, nothing due yet) and the end-of-year estimate built from those same records. The comfort is knowing early — never present the estimate as a promise; it's a good guide that updates as records are added. Audience is non-tax-savvy; keep it to one idea.

0:00–0:10 The hook — stop guessing 23 words · ~10s

On screen

Open on the worry, kept light: a scribbled "put aside for tax??" note, or a savings account named "TAX MAYBE" — your creative call. Cut to the Tax Planner page loading.

Voiceover

How much should you be putting aside for tax? For most people that's a guess. The Tax Planner turns it into a number.

On screen

Voiceover

It starts with where you are now. One quarter in, your records show £9,128.86 of profit — and no tax due yet, because you're still inside your personal allowance.

Then it looks ahead. Carry on at this rate and the year comes out around £36,515 of income — with roughly £6,225 of tax and National Insurance to pay. Now you know what to put aside each month.

0:38–0:47 The closer — a guide, not a bill 21 words · ~9s

On screen

Add one record in the ledger → cut back to the planner, the estimate nudges → end card: FileThat logo + filethat.co.uk.

Voiceover

It's an estimate, not a bill — a good guide that updates itself every time you add a record. No January surprises.

Fixed claims — please don't paraphrase these

· "It's an estimate, not a bill — a good guide" — keep the hedge; never present the year-end figure as what the viewer will pay, and never round it into a promise ("you'll owe £6,225").

· "No tax due yet, because you're still inside your personal allowance" — keep tied to this demo's figures; do not generalise into advice about anyone else's allowance.

· "Updates itself every time you add a record" — keep as written; do not strengthen to "always accurate" or "real-time HMRC figures".

· No pricing in this video, and no specific tax-saving promises.

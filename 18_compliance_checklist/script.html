Fixed claims — please don't paraphrase these

· "You'll always know what's next" — the promise is orientation, not automation. Don't drift into "FileThat files for you" territory.

· Don't voice any deadline numbers ("due in 97 days") — they're true only on the recording day. The screen can show them; the VO stays general.

· No pricing in this video. If an edit wants a price line, lift the exact wording from Video 1 ("keeping your records is free") — nothing new.

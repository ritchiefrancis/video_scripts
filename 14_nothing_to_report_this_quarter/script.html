Nothing to Report This Quarter

Target 0:30 VO ≈ 71 words The nil-update video British English

The brief: nil updates — a quiet quarter still means filing, and here's how little that is. Thirty seconds, short and reassuring: same review-and-submit flow as any other quarter, just with nothing in it. This video exists so nobody panics (or silently skips a deadline) the first time they have a quarter with no work. Audience is non-tax-savvy; no jargon, no lecture about penalties — the tone is "this is easy", not "you must".

0:00–0:09 The hook — quiet quarter, quick job 21 words · ~9s

On screen

Self Assessment → Your Filing Deadlines: the open quarter (6 Apr – 5 Jul 2026, due 7 Aug 2026) waiting in the list. Calm, unhurried framing — nothing flashing, nothing red.

Voiceover

Quiet quarter — no work, no income? HMRC still expects an update. The good news: a nil update takes about a minute.

Footage note: the open-obligation row is the same staged screen as Videos 1, 5 and 7 — record once, reuse.

0:09–0:21 The nil update — same flow, empty figures 28 words · ~12s

Open the quarter → the update form loads with £0.00 income and £0.00 expenses (nothing in the records, so nothing in the form) → a beat to "review" → press Submit.

Voiceover

It's exactly the same flow as any other quarter: open the update, and because your records show nothing, the figures show nothing. Have a glance, press submit — done.

Footage note: the staged demo quarter normally has real figures in it (£15,750 income etc.) — load demo scene 14 right before this shot: it empties the records and flips the HMRC mock so the form genuinely shows zeros. Don't reuse the pre-filled shot here, and load any other scene afterwards to bring the staged figures back.

0:21–0:31 The closer — deadline met, carry on 22 words · ~10s

On screen

Confirmation on screen → Your Filing Deadlines with the quarter showing as met → end card: FileThat logo + filethat.co.uk.

Voiceover

That's the deadline met and nothing hanging over you. And when work picks up again, you just carry on recording as normal.

Fixed claims — please don't paraphrase these

· "HMRC still expects an update" — keep it matter-of-fact; no penalty warnings or scare lines in any cut.

· "Have a glance, press submit" — the glance is the review step; keep it in the VO and in frame. Never cut to "it submits itself".

· "Takes about a minute" — keep the hedge word "about"; don't tighten it to a promise ("under 60 seconds").

· No pricing in this video. If an edit wants a price line, lift the exact wording from Video 1 ("keeping your records is free") — nothing new.

Practicalities

  • 1080p minimum, 16:9, MP4. Clean voiceover mix; VO as a separate stem if possible; SRT captions appreciated.
  • Total VO ≈ 71 words — lands right on 0:30 at a relaxed pace (~2.4 words per second). Please don't pad it; the brevity is the message.
  • All demo screens and data will be staged and recorded by me before the session (including the extra nil-state pass noted in beat 2) — everything is HMRC's test environment; nothing on screen is a live submission.

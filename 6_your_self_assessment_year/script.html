01Purpose & tone

This is a tutorial, not an advert. The viewer has an account and wants to know what they have to do and when. Tone matches FileThat's voice: plain-spoken, honest, reassuring — closer to a knowledgeable friend than a corporate explainer. British English throughout ("HMRC", "sole trader").

The script is written to be read as-is at a natural pace (~150 wpm). Light rewording to suit the presenter's voice is fine; the two lines marked keep verbatim carry compliance meaning and should stay as written.

02The script — beat by beat

0:00–0:301 · The shape of the year~72 words
Screen
Self Assessment → Your Filing Deadlines — slow pan down the obligations table (quarters, due dates, met/open) → cut to Filing Checklist
VO

Under Making Tax Digital, your Self Assessment year has a simple shape: four quarterly updates, then one final declaration to sign the year off. Your Filing Deadlines shows every quarter and its due date — met or still open — and the Filing Checklist tracks where you are in the journey at any moment. If you're ever unsure what's next, start at these two pages.

0:30–1:102 · A quarterly update, from your records~95 words
Screen
Your Filing Deadlines → open the due quarter → form loads → HOLD on the "Pre-filled from digital records" banner (fields visibly locked) → scroll Income and Expenses tabs → Submit → success state
VO

When a quarter's due, open it — and notice you're not typing numbers in. The figures are filled in from your digital records, and the fields are read-only. That's deliberate, and it's what HMRC means by digital record-keeping: what you submit is your records. Need to change a figure? Change the record, and the form follows. And because updates are cumulative — each one covers the year so far — a correction simply rolls into your next update. Review, submit, done.

Note

"The fields are read-only… what you submit is your records" — keep verbatim. The on-screen banner says the same thing; let the camera linger on it.

1:10–1:303 · Check what you owe, any time~45 words
Screen
Self Assessment → Calculate & Submit → Calculation Type: "In Year" → trigger → calculation summary appears
VO

After any update, you can ask HMRC for an in-year calculation — a running estimate of the year's tax so far. No more waiting until January to find out the damage. Little and often, no surprises.

1:30–2:054 · Everything that isn't day-to-day trading~85 words
Screen
Self Assessment → Extra Details — slow scroll through the menu tree, pacing the scroll to the buckets as they're spoken (viewers pause here to find their item)
VO

Before you finalise your year, this is where everything else goes — anything that isn't your day-to-day trading. Other income: employment, savings interest, dividends, state benefits. Reliefs: pension contributions, charitable giving, investments. CIS deductions if you're in construction. Capital gains if you've sold something. You don't need most of these — find the ones that apply to you, and any time before your final declaration is the right time.

2:05–2:205 · Year-end allowances and adjustments~40 words
Screen
Self Assessment → Year-End Allowances
VO

After your final quarter, if you have capital allowances or accounting adjustments, they're made once, here. Plenty of people will have nothing to do on this page — it's there if the year needs it.

Note

This page is optional by design for many users — keep the reassuring tone; don't make it sound like a required step.

2:20–2:506 · Signing off the year~80 words
Screen
Calculate & Submit → Calculation Type: "Intent To Finalise" → review returned calculation → Final Declaration dialog → confirm → cut to Proof of Submission (receipt visible)
VO

Then the finale. Trigger an Intent to Finalise — think of it as the dress rehearsal: HMRC checks everything and returns your final figures. Review them, and when you're happy, submit your Final Declaration. That's you signing off the year — the Making Tax Digital equivalent of filing your return. Every step gets a receipt under Proof of Submission: filed, and proven.

Note

"That's you signing off the year — the Making Tax Digital equivalent of filing your return" — keep verbatim.

2:50–2:557 · Closer~40 words
Screen
Filing Checklist — everything complete/green
VO

If plans change after you've declared, Intent to Amend reopens the year while HMRC's window is open. But that's another video. Four updates, one declaration — that's Self Assessment in FileThat.

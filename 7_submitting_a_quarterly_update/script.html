Submitting a Quarterly Update

Target 0:45 VO ≈ 107 words The at-the-moment task British English

The brief: the at-the-moment task version of the quarterly update — for the viewer whose quarter has just ended and who wants to get it done right now. Open the due quarter, see the figures already filled in from their records, review, submit, confirmation. Video 6 explains the whole year; this one is just the job in hand. The audience is non-tax-savvy; no jargon, no detours. Keep it brisk and calm — the whole point is how small this task is.

0:00–0:08 The nudge — a quarter is due 19 words · ~8s

On screen

Self Assessment → Your Filing Deadlines. The table shows the 2026–27 quarter 6 Apr – 5 Jul 2026 as open, due 7 Aug 2026, with last year's quarters all showing as met below it. Hold briefly on the open row.

Voiceover

The quarter's just ended, and FileThat's telling you an update is due. Here's how little there is to do.

Open it, and the first thing you'll notice: the figures are already filled in. Your income, your expenses, your profit for the quarter — all added up from the records you've been keeping. You don't type a number.

0:24–0:37 Review — the form follows the records 29 words · ~13s

Have a proper look before you go on. If something's not right, you don't edit the form — you fix the record it came from, and the figure updates itself.

Voiceover

Happy with it? Press submit, and off it goes to HMRC. You'll get a confirmation on screen — and that's the quarter done.

Fixed claims — please don't paraphrase these

· "You don't type a number" refers to this update form — its fields are read-only, filled from records. Don't broaden it to "no data entry ever"; the records themselves still get added week by week (that's Video 5).

· The review step is the point of beat 3 — never cut it so the video reads as "it submits itself". The viewer always reviews and presses submit.

· "Off it goes to HMRC. You'll get a confirmation on screen" — keep as written; don't add promises about HMRC processing times or acceptance.

· Every figure and date on screen (£15,750.00 income, £6,621.14 expenses, £9,128.86 profit, quarter 6 Apr – 5 Jul 2026, due 7 Aug 2026) comes from the staged demo data — please don't substitute round numbers in captions or thumbnails.

· No pricing in this video. If an edit wants a price line, lift the exact wording from Video 1 ("keeping your records is free") — nothing new.

Practicalities

  • 1080p minimum, 16:9, MP4. Clean voiceover mix; VO as a separate stem if possible; SRT captions appreciated.
  • Total VO ≈ 107 words — lands around 0:45 at a relaxed pace (~2.4 words per second), right on target. If it runs long in the edit, the caption in beat 3 can carry that beat with a shorter VO line.
  • All demo screens and data will be staged and recorded by me before the session — the open quarter, the pre-filled figures and the confirmation are all against HMRC's test environment; nothing on screen is a live submission.

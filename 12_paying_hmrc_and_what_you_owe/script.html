Paying HMRC & What You Owe

Target 1:00 VO ≈ 154 words The no-surprises video British English

The brief: where liabilities and payments live. The emotional job of this video is the "why am I paying half of next year already?" reassurance — payments on account explained in plain words, without a single scary term left undefined. Show the what-you-owe screen, the payment history, a quick VAT nod, and point at the Tax Planner for "how much to put aside". Important: FileThat shows what's owed; the paying itself happens with HMRC — never imply you can pay inside the app. Audience is non-tax-savvy; no jargon.

Fixed claims — please don't paraphrase these

· "The paying itself happens with HMRC" — keep this in every cut. Never show or imply a pay button inside FileThat; the app shows what's owed and when, HMRC takes the money.

· "A head start on next year's tax" — keep the payments-on-account explanation this plain. Don't add rules about thresholds, who does and doesn't pay them, or how they're calculated; that's beyond this video.

· All amounts and dates (£6,286.80, two × £3,143.40, £9,430.20 payable 31 Jan 2027, £3,143.40 due 31 Jul 2027, £4,850.00 paid 25 Jan 2026, VAT £2,273.51 due 7 Aug 2026) are staged demo data — use them exactly as shown, in captions and thumbnails too.

· No pricing in this video. If an edit wants a price line, lift the exact wording from Video 1 ("keeping your records is free") — nothing new.

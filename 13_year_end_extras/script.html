Year-End Extras

Target 1:00 VO ≈ 156 words The "you probably need none of these" video British English

The brief: the Extra Details sections and Year-End Allowances — everything beyond the four quarterly updates, on the way to the final declaration. The single most important message is reassurance: most people need few or none of these, and skipping them is fine. Keep the scope modest — this is a tour of where things go, not a tax lesson. The audience is non-tax-savvy; no jargon, and nothing that makes the year-end feel bigger than it is.

Voiceover

Your quarterly updates cover the day-to-day. But before you sign the year off, there's one last stop: the extras. Don't worry — for most people it's a short visit.

Fixed claims — please don't paraphrase these

· "Plenty of people skip the lot" / "if none apply, there's nothing to do" — the reassurance is the video. Never let an edit make Extra Details or Year-End Allowances sound like required steps.

· "Review the figures, press submit" — keep the review step in the closer; never promise the final declaration files itself without review.

· Keep the allowance example generic in the VO ("things like a worn-out sofa or fridge") — the £420.00 figure stays on-screen only; no £ amounts or allowance rules spoken aloud.

· Dates on screen (final declaration due 31 Jan 2027, year 2025–26) are staged demo data — use them exactly as shown.

· No pricing in this video. If an edit wants a price line, lift the exact wording from Video 1 ("keeping your records is free") — nothing new.
